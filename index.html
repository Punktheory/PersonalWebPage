Supervised by Dr. Chi-Keung Tang

FLAIR: Controllable Surgical Video Generation

Reference-free surgical video generation using flow-derived latent action priors. FLAIR predicts action priors from text and injects them into a frozen diffusion transformer to produce stable, action-consistent motion.

  • Evaluated across Wan-2.1, CogVideoX-2B, and HunyuanVideo-1.5 backbones.
  • Contributed to SurgActionClip-30K, an action-centric surgical video dataset spanning 11 source datasets with structured action–instrument–target captions.
  • Contributed to SurgMetrics, a surgical-domain evaluation suite with six artifact-specific axes aligned with human judgments.

Undergraduate Research Opportunity Program · HKUST

June 2025 – Present

Generative AI · Supervised by Dr. Qifeng Chen and Dr. Runtao Liu

LiveStockArena: A Live Benchmark for Financial Forecasting

A dynamic benchmark evaluating language models against live financial markets under shared conditions. Using future market outcomes helps limit data contamination and tests real-time forecasting ability.

  • Collected more than 5,000 results across five months and built a public leaderboard.
  • Ongoing work extends the benchmark to AI agents and evaluates their ability to plan and execute trades.

SafetyDPO: Safety Alignment for Text-to-Image Generation

Worked as an undergraduate research assistant on applying Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and LoRA experts to improve the safety of Stable Diffusion models while retaining generative capability.

  • Experiments on Stable Diffusion 1.5 achieved a 93% appropriate-generation rate, compared with a 70% baseline.
  • Continued implementation and experimentation on Stable Diffusion 3.5.

Contribution: undergraduate research assistant. The project paper was accepted to ICCV 2025; I am not a paper author. Project code
